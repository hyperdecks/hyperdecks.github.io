Resource Description Framework (RDF)

-Framework for making statements about (Web) resources in the form of triples

-Triples induce a labeled directed multi-graph (“GGG”)

-Stored in Triplestores or Quadstores

-Multiple serialization formats

RDF does not make any assumptions about what resources are. It is therefore typically used in conjunction with vocabularies that provide these semantics.

Different Serializations of RDF

-RDF/XML (first one)

-JSON-LD (JSON with context)

-Turtle (compact, readable)

-N-Triples (line-based, easy parsing, large)

-N-Quads (context for each triple)

-Notation3/N3 (support for rules)

Ontologies are a means to make an explicit commitment to shared meaning among a community

-Model of (some aspects of) the world or a specific domain (“domain ontology”)

-Defines vocabulary relevant to a domain with intended meaning of domain concepts

-Gives taxonomy of concepts in the vocabulary (classes and their relationships)

-Gives relationships among these concepts (triples)

Components

-Set of axioms describing structure of the model (schema)

-Set of facts describing some particular concrete situation (instances)

-(Set of rules for reasoning)

Trade-offs

-Deep ontologies are highly expressive, but require considerable effort to build (e.g., sosa:Sensor)

-Shallow ontologies are broad rather than deep and much less expressive (e.g., foaf:Person)

-Folksonomies are created by end users, largely ambiguous and processed best-effort / statistically (e.g., TikTok tags)
