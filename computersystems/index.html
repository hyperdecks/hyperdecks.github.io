Lectures

Wednesdays, 10-12, room 61-152.

Tuesday slots (9-10, room 61-152) continue, repeat or deepen the lecture, or support the exercises; they are announced the week before.

Exercises and exam

Assignments in teams of two on loaned Raspberry Pi 400 hardware: 20% of the grade.

Final exam after the winter holidays, 60 minutes, closed book (TI-30-series calculators only): 80% of the grade.
